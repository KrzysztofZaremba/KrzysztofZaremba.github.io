<section class="kz-section">
  <h2 class="kz-section-title">Research interests</h2>
  <div class="kz-cols">
    <div>
      <h3>Health economics</h3>
      <p>Pharmaceutical policy and drug subsidies, medication shortages, physician decision-making, and the spread of infectious disease.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3>Networks</h3>
      <p>How commuting and social networks transmit disease and shape behaviour, and how to identify peer effects when network data are noisy.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3>Family economics</h3>
      <p>Marriage and dating markets, sex ratios, fertility, and the health of mothers and newborns.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
